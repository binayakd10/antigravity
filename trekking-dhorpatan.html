Dhorpatan Hunting Reserve & Highlands

An immersive journey through Nepal's only hunting reserve, sprawling across high alpine meadows, oak and rhododendron forests, and traditional shepherd settlements below the Dhaulagiri range. Known for its untamed biodiversity, roaming herds of Himalayan blue sheep (Naur), and serene grasslands.

ELEVATION RANGE 2,850m – 3,900m
REGION Baglung / Rukum, Nepal
WILDLIFE HABITAT Blue Sheep (Naur)
TERRAIN Alpine Pastures & Marsh

Alpine Meadows & Shepherd Life in Dhorpatan

Captured in the vast green pastures of Dhorpatan, where grazing herds of mountain sheep and goats roam freely amidst rolling hill fogs. Experiencing traditional highland shepherd lifestyles and pristine natural tranquility.
