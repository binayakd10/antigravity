HIMALAYAN TREKKING EXPEDITION

North Annapurna Base Camp & Panchakunda Lake

A high-altitude expedition across the rugged wilderness of Narchyang, Myagdi, leading to the North Face of Mount Annapurna I (8,091m) and the pristine glacial waters of Panchakunda Lake (4,050m). Reaching North ABC at 4,190 meters tests physical stamina, resilience, and fosters an enduring respect for nature's monumental scale.

MAX ALTITUDE 4,190m / 13,746ft
LOCATION Narchyang, Myagdi, Nepal
GLACIAL LAKE Panchakunda (4,050m)
COORDINATES 28°31'48" N, 83°52'41" E
